### Alexander Michels

Assistant Professor, School of Economic, Political and Policy Sciences, The University of Texas at Dallas

Dr. Michels specializes in geospatial data science and cyberGIS to tackle societal challenges. He earned his Ph.D. in Informatics (2025) and M.S. in Geography
(2024) from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign under Dr. Shaowen Wang, and a B.S. in Mathematics and Financial Economics from Westminster College
(2019). His research interests include geospatial data science, cyberGIS, spatial accessibility analysis, and high-performance computing for geographic problems.

He received the William L. Garrison Award for Best Dissertation in Computational Geography and is Vice Chair of the AAG Cyberinfrastructure Specialty Group.
