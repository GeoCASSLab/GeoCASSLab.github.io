projects

Research projects of the GeoCASS Lab.

Research Cyberinfrastructure

Democratizing HPC and reproducible science gateways for geospatial research, including CyberGIS-Compute and CyberGISX.

Health Geography

Geographic approaches to health and healthcare, including spatial accessibility to healthcare resources and disease and vulnerability analysis.

GeoAI

AI and machine learning for geospatial science and urban informatics, including the NSF-funded CITY-AI CyberTraining project.

Algorithms and Methods for Spatial Accessibility

Developing novel algorithms and methods for accurate and scalable spatial accessibility analyses.

Agent-Based Modeling

Spatially explicit agent-based models, including calibration, sensitivity analysis, reproducibility, and applications in public health and land markets.
