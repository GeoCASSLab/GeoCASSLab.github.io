Director

Alexander Michels

Director, GeoCASS Lab

Assistant Professor, School of Economic, Political and Policy Sciences, The University of Texas at Dallas

Dr. Michels specializes in geospatial data science and cyberGIS to tackle societal challenges. He earned his Ph.D. in Informatics (2025) and M.S. in Geography (2024) from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign under Dr. Shaowen Wang, and a B.S. in Mathematics and Financial Economics from Westminster College (2019). His research interests include geospatial data science, cyberGIS, spatial accessibility analysis, and high-performance computing for geographic problems.

He received the William L. Garrison Award for Best Dissertation in Computational Geography and is Vice Chair of the AAG Cyberinfrastructure Specialty Group.

800 W. Campbell Road, GR 31, Richardson, TX 75080

Graduate Students

Americo Gamarra

PhD Student, Geospatial Information Science

Americo is a Ph.D. student in Geospatial Information Sciences at The University of Texas at Dallas. He holds a M.S. in GIS from Penn State University. His research lies at the intersection of GIScience, remote sensing, and data science, with a focus on applying machine learning and deep learning to detect and map oil and gas infrastructure from remotely sensed imagery. His hands-on experience includes spatial databases, web GIS, and the development of mapping tools and applications in the ArcGIS ecosystem using Python, JavaScript, and C#.

Americo.Gamarra@UTDallas.edu

Kwadwo Frimpong

PhD Student, Geospatial Information Science

My research lies at the intersection of GIScience, remote sensing, and climate change adaptation, with a focus on land use/cover change, land surface temperature, heat stress, and thermal comfort in urban environments. My work integrates machine learning, spatial statistics, and climate indices to examine the impacts of global and local drivers on heat stress exposure and resilience.
